How this works. It reads every posted pairing and result, rebuilds the standings with Omnidex's own tiebreakers (points, opponents' match win %, game win %, opponents' game win %), then plays out everything still unknown thousands of times. Unfinished matches are treated as even, with draws and game scores at this season's observed rates. Rounds that are not paired yet are paired by points without rematches.

The maths card assumes standard Swiss pairing: players meet others on the same points, an odd group pairs one player down, and an odd field gives a bye. "Most who can reach it" is the largest number of players who could finish on that score or better.

Percentages are estimates, not guarantees, until the last result is in. Checked against 39 completed events this season: the rebuilt final top 8 matched Omnidex every time.
